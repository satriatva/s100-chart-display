Depth & Tide

S-102 Depth Probe

Hover to read depth under the cursor; click the chart to pin a reading. Works together with the tide tool.

–

S-104 Tide at a Station

Click the chart near an S-104 grid point to plot its water-level curve.

Water Level Adjustment (S-102 + S-104)

Applies the S-104 water level at the chosen time to the S-102 depths.

––

–

Actual water depth = S-102 charted depth + S-104 water level (both relative to chart datum). Lowest / Highest use the last clicked tide station (or the map centre).
